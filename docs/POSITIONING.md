# Positioning

> Last Updated: 2026-11-25

Working positioning for camsync (a codename; see [`NAMING.md`](./NAMING.md)). It states what is true
today and where the tool is meant to go. It is the input to the landing's design process and to the
brand search, not marketing copy.

## In one sentence

**camsync lets a person see every camera at once and decide how they are woven into one vertical
video, while each camera keeps its own full-quality recording.**

## What is true today (measured 2026-11-24, one iPhone 15)

- An iPhone in its browser records the full sensor picture, upright, at 2160x3840 and 30 frames per
  second, or 4:3 at 2160x2880.
- A computer on the same local network sees it live at 30 frames per second, and starts and stops the
  recording.
- The original lands on the computer while it is being recorded, in one-second pieces.

## Where it is meant to go (RFC 0073, accepted 2026-11-24)

1. **Many sources, one program.** Several cameras and screens, seen together and switched live.
2. **The decision is the record.** What the operator chooses (scene, title, cut) is written down with
   the exact frame it happened on, not burned into the picture. The finished video is rendered later,
   at full quality, from the originals and those decisions.
3. **Finished on MADFAM's servers.** The render, captions, sound and every social format are produced
   by MADFAM's own production platform, not on the operator's computer.
4. **Part of the ecosystem.** Integrations with MADFAM's other platforms, where they help.
5. **Masks near real time** (owner direction 2026-11-24, ruled 2026-11-25). A person or object is
   cut out live on the phone and seen on the computer; the finished mask is made on the servers from
   the original and the operator's choices, so it can be made again later with a better model. Only
   models whose licences fit camsync's own are used.

## Who it is for first

MADFAM's own production work (owner ruling, 2026-11-24): the people making MADFAM's vertical videos
with phones and a computer.

## What makes it different

| Usual desktop studio | camsync |
| --- | --- |
| The computer composites and encodes every frame, live | The computer only previews and records decisions; the cameras keep their originals |
| The output is fixed when you press stop | The output can be re-rendered from the originals and the decisions |
| Built around a horizontal screen | Built around the vertical frame social video uses |
| A phone joins as a webcam, cropped to its shape | A phone records its full picture in the orientation it is held |

## What it is not

- Not a surveillance tool. Every source is put there on purpose by the people in it, and what is seen
  is meant to be shown. This is the line the brand must hold.
- Not live streaming to platforms, for now (RFC 0073, CS-2).
- Not a finished product: it is a working prototype, and the landing says so.

## Voice

Plain, specific, measured. Say what exists and what does not. Spanish first, English second.
