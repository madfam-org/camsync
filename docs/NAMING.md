# Naming

> Last Updated: 2026-11-25

**camsync is a codename.** It names the tool, the repository, the services and the host
`camsync.madfam.io` until a public brand is chosen (owner direction, 2026-11-25: «continue working with
the codename camsync at camsync.madfam.io»). Nothing public should treat it as the final brand: no
logo, no tagline built on the word, no trademark filing.

This file is the public record of the search for that brand. The register evidence behind it, record by
record, is kept in MADFAM's private operations repository.

## The brief

In the owner's words (2026-11-24):

- The platform should eventually replace a full desktop live-production studio, "multiple AV sources
  and all", and grow integrations across MADFAM's ecosystem.
- The name should convey **"convergent vision and the ability to choose how to weave that vision."**
- The idea is close to a panopticon, **without the "big brother" connotation**: many views seen from
  one place, by someone who chose them, with nothing hidden.
- **Mexico first.** A candidate is screened against Mexico's trademark register (IMPI) before anything
  else.

## How a candidate is screened

In this order, stopping at the first serious problem:

1. **IMPI's register**, classes 9 (software), 38 (transmission and streaming), 41 (video production and
   entertainment) and 42 (software as a service), by sound as well as spelling. Each record that could
   block is opened: the register's own "Registrada" and "En trámite" labels stay on marks that have
   expired or been abandoned, so the end date and the notices are what count.
2. **Meaning and association in Mexico:** surnames, public figures, slang, and meanings in other
   languages.
3. **Origin:** no word taken from the living culture of an indigenous people without that people's
   consent. Mexico's federal law on the cultural heritage of indigenous and Afro-Mexican peoples (2022)
   reserves that heritage to the community.
4. **Collisions:** existing products with the name, nearest first in live video and streaming.
5. **Domains and handles**, through MADFAM's own LexiDrop.

| FOVEA | Identical marks registered at IMPI in classes 9 and 42 |
| TANTU | Identical marks registered at IMPI |
